Scene 1: Introduction and Definition of Biology
Etymological origins from Greek bios (life) and logos (study). Biology as the empirical natural science investigating living organisms, their structure, function, and vital processes.

Scene 2: Universal Characteristics of Living Organisms
Cellular organization, metabolism (anabolism vs catabolism), and homeostasis. Living things maintain steady internal states and convert energy.

Scene 3: Growth, Reproduction, and Genetic Transmission
Irreversible biological growth, asexual vs sexual reproduction, DNA hereditary transmission, and evolutionary adaptation in populations.

Scene 4: Borderline Entities: Viruses, Viroids, and Prions
Virions as non-cellular macromolecular complexes. Obligate intracellular parasites with no independent metabolism outside hosts.

Scene 5: Hierarchical Levels of Biological Organization
Ascending biological hierarchy: Subatomic, atoms (CHNOPS), molecules, organelles, cells, tissues, organs, ecosystems, biosphere. Emergent properties.

Scene 6: Major Branches and Sub-disciplines of Biology
Structural (cytology, histology, anatomy, physiology), taxonomic (botany, zoology, microbiology), and integrative fields (genetics, ecology, biotechnology).

Scene 7: The Scientific Method in Biological Investigations
Systematic inquiry: Observation, question, falsifiable hypothesis, controlled experiments, independent/dependent/controlled variables, conclusion.

Scene 8: Added Gap 1: Endemic Afroalpine Wildlife Conservation
Walia ibex in Semien Mountains, Ethiopian wolf in Bale Mountains, Gelada baboon in Menz. High-altitude hypoxia adaptation, population bottlenecks, in situ conservation.

Scene 9: Added Gap 2: Crop Wild Relatives & Vavilov Center
Nikolai Vavilov's 8 diversity centers. Teff (Eragrostis tef), Coffee Arabica wild cloud forest alleles, Enset fermentation (Kocho), and EBI gene bank.

Scene 10: Added Gap 3: Experimental Design & Laboratory Bio-Safety
Compound light microscope optics, total magnification formula, PPE safety, international biohazard symbols, and autoclaving waste disposal.

Scene 11: Section Summary & Ethiopian Biodiversity Heritage
Synthesis of core biological concepts, living criteria, and practical applications for Ethiopian development and conservation.
