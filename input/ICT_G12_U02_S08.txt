Analysis and Solution. This scenario demonstrates the integration of IoT, Fog Computing, and Cloud Computing. IoT soil moisture sensors and weather stations would be deployed across the farms to collect environmental data.

Analysis and Solution. This is a classic Big Data problem. Ethio Telecom generates vast volumes of data at high velocity from its network infrastructure. The solution involves building a Big Data analytics platform on cloud infrastructure capable of ingesting, storing, and processing streaming network data.

Analysis and Solution. The ECX processes structured transaction data including commodity grades, volumes, prices, buyer and seller information, and timestamps. This data, combined with external data sources such as satellite weather imagery, international coffee price indices from the New York and London exchanges, and production forecasts from the Ministry of Agriculture, constitutes a rich Big Data resource.
