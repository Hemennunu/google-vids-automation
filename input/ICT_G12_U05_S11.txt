This section provides supplementary content aligned with the Cambridge AS Level Information Technology syllabus. It extends the Grade 12 Ethiopian curriculum to cover additional depth expected at the Cambridge AS Level.

The Cambridge AS Level syllabus expects students to understand not only the basic troubleshooting process but also the theoretical frameworks that underpin systematic problem-solving. One such framework is the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model, which divides network communication into seven layers: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, and Application.

Another important concept is the difference between fault management and performance management. Fault management focuses on detecting, isolating, and correcting abnormal network operations, while performance management involves monitoring and optimising network performance to meet service level agreements.

Beyond the basic tools covered in the Grade 12 curriculum, AS Level students should also be familiar with the arp command, which displays and modifies the Address Resolution Protocol cache used to map IP addresses to physical MAC addresses. The route command displays and modifies the local IP routing table.

At the Cambridge AS Level, preventive maintenance is approached strategically. Students learn to develop maintenance schedules based on factors such as equipment age, usage patterns, criticality of systems, and manufacturer recommendations. A preventive maintenance plan includes regular inspection schedules, software update policies, backup procedures, and documentation standards.
