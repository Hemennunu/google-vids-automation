# Section 2: Learning Objectives



## Definition

The diagram turns the objectives into a path from defining new technologies to applying and evaluating them responsibly.



## Lead

By the end of this unit, we will be able to differentiate between narrow AI, general AI, and superintelligence, and evaluate the current state of AI development. We will analyse the relationship between artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning, and evaluate the potential benefits and ethical risks of AI applications in Ethiopian contexts. We will also distinguish between Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, and Mixed Reality, construct a reasoned analysis of how data science enables organisations to extract value from large datasets, and evaluate the characteristics of big data using the 5 Vs framework. Finally, we will design a basic data science investigation plan for a given Ethiopian problem, and critically assess the ethical implications of AI and data science applications — including issues of bias, privacy, and digital inequality in the African context.



## Paragraphs

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems, including learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. AI has transitioned from a theoretical concept to a transformative force reshaping industries, professions, and daily life worldwide.

Machine learning (ML) is the dominant paradigm within AI, enabling systems to learn from data rather than being explicitly programmed. In supervised learning, a model is trained on labelled examples and learns to predict outputs for new inputs. In unsupervised learning, the model discovers patterns in unlabelled data. In reinforcement learning, an agent learns by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties for its actions. Deep learning, using artificial neural networks with many layers, has achieved remarkable results in image recognition, natural language processing, and game-playing.

Data science is the interdisciplinary field that extracts insights from large and complex datasets using statistical methods, machine learning, and computational tools. The data science workflow includes problem formulation, data collection, data cleaning and preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, model building, evaluation, and deployment. Python is the primary programming language, with key libraries including Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn, and TensorFlow.

The Internet of Things (IoT) connects physical devices — sensors, appliances, vehicles — to the internet, enabling data collection and exchange. Smart agriculture applications use soil moisture sensors, weather stations, and drone imagery to optimise farming, directly relevant to Ethiopia's agricultural development challenges. Blockchain technology creates distributed, immutable ledgers without a trusted central authority; the Ethiopian government has partnered with IOHK to create blockchain-based digital student identity cards.

Ethical considerations are inseparable from AI deployment. Algorithmic bias occurs when AI systems reflect or amplify biases present in training data, leading to discriminatory outcomes. Privacy concerns arise from massive data collection requirements. Job displacement from automation requires policy responses in education and social protection. Responsible AI development requires transparency, fairness, accountability, and human oversight — principles that must guide Ethiopia's digital transformation.
