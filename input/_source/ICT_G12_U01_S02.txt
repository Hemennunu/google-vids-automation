# Unit 1: Learning Objectives



## Definition

The path turns the unit objectives into a sequence from analysis and protection to collaboration and citizenship.



## Lead

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:



## Paragraphs

Identify and explain the ethical and legal issues in the use of information systems, including privacy, information security, digital ownership and information gathering.

Analyse the social issues caused by information systems, including socialisation gaps, cyberbullying, social media and gaming addiction, and the impact of automation on employment.

Describe the environmental and health issues arising from the use of information systems, such as e-waste, energy consumption and ergonomic health concerns.

Define intellectual property and distinguish between the four main types of intellectual property rights: copyrights, patents, trademarks and trade secrets, with reference to the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office.

Explain the concept of digital identity management and describe how individuals and nations, including Ethiopia through the National ID project, manage digital identities securely.

Demonstrate an understanding of digital collaboration and identify the key components, tools and technologies that facilitate effective collaboration in educational and professional settings.

Evaluate the role of digital citizenship and describe how individuals can engage responsibly and effectively in civic and national affairs using digital technologies.

Apply the knowledge gained to real-world scenarios, including the use of collaboration tools and the practice of ethical digital behaviour in the Ethiopian context.
