Lesson content (authoritative — stay accurate and do not add facts beyond it):
This unit covers Emerging Technologies as part of the Grade 11 ICT curriculum, aligned with the Ethiopian MoE 2023 standard textbook.

Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) are two closely related but distinct technologies that alter human perception of reality through computer-generated content. Virtual Reality creates a completely artificial environment that immerses the user in a simulated world, replacing the real-world environment entirely.

The key difference between AR and VR lies in their relationship with the real world. VR completely replaces reality with a simulated environment, requiring immersive headsets such as the Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, or PlayStation VR. AR enhances reality by adding digital elements to the live view, requiring only a camera-equipped device such as a smartphone or tablet.

The applications of AR and VR span numerous fields. In education, VR enables students to take virtual field trips to historical sites, explore the human body in three dimensions, or conduct virtual science experiments without the need for expensive laboratory equipment. AR enhances textbooks with interactive 3D models that appear when scanned with a smartphone.
