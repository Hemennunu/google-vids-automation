This unit introduces us to the discipline of software engineering — the systematic, principled approach to designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software systems. Software is the intellectual engine of the digital age: every application used on a smartphone, every banking system that processes transactions, every hospital record system that stores patient data, and every e-government portal that delivers public services is the product of software engineering. In this unit, we will move beyond the introductory programming concepts of earlier grades and engage with software development at a professional and theoretical level.

Ethiopia's technology sector is growing at an extraordinary pace. Companies such as Gebeya, iCog Labs, Apposit, and MyMarian Platform are building software products and platforms that serve both Ethiopian and international markets.
