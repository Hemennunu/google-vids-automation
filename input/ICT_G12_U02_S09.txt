Lesson content (authoritative — stay accurate and do not add facts beyond it):
In this practical investigation, you will work with a simulated IoT sensor dataset representing environmental data collected from a smart agriculture deployment in Ethiopia. The objective is to apply your understanding of Big Data analysis concepts, cloud-based data processing, and IoT data interpretation to extract meaningful insights. This activity can be completed using spreadsheet software such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, or using a programming language such as Python if available.

The dataset, which you will create based on the specifications below, contains readings from IoT sensors deployed across three farm sites in the Oromia, SNNP, and Amhara regions over a period of thirty days. Each record includes the following fields: date, farm site identifier, soil moisture percentage, air temperature in degrees Celsius, humidity percentage, rainfall in millimetres, and sunlight hours.

Your investigation will be assessed on the quality of your dataset design, the appropriateness of your data cleaning methods, the accuracy and clarity of your descriptive analysis, the depth of insight in your pattern identification, the practicality of your recommendations, and the professionalism of your written report.

Tone: Clear, warm and encouraging, suitable for secondary-school students. Use simple language.
