## Definition

## Lead

Instructions: Answer ONE question only — either D1 or D2. Your essay should be well-structured, with a clear introduction, developed arguments supported by evidence or examples, and a conclusion. Ethiopian and global ICT examples are expected. Aim for approximately 500–700 words.



## Paragraphs

"The rapid adoption of artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and the Internet of Things presents Ethiopia with transformative economic opportunities but also poses significant risks to privacy, employment, and national security. Effective policy and technological governance are therefore essential."

Using your knowledge of ICT and examples drawn from Ethiopia and other countries, evaluate the extent to which the benefits of these three emerging technologies outweigh their risks for Ethiopian society and the economy.

"Securing Ethiopia's Critical Digital Infrastructure"

The Ethiopian government is planning to digitise core public services — including land registration, tax collection, national ID management, and health records — into a unified national digital platform. The system will be hosted in a hybrid cloud environment (partly on government-owned servers, partly on a public cloud provider).

As a senior ICT consultant, write a report evaluating the cybersecurity challenges and system design considerations for this platform.



## List items

Analyse the economic and social benefits of AI, cloud computing, and IoT in the Ethiopian context

Evaluate at least THREE specific risks (technical, ethical, or socio-economic)

Assess the role of policy frameworks such as Ethiopia's Digital Economy Strategy and data protection laws

Reach a justified conclusion as to whether the benefits outweigh the risks, or vice versa

The key cybersecurity threats to a national-scale digital platform (with reference to specific attack types)

A layered security architecture (Defence in Depth) appropriate for this system

The advantages and disadvantages of a hybrid cloud architecture for sensitive government data

The role of legal and regulatory frameworks (e.g., Ethiopia's Computer Crime Proclamation, GDPR principles) in protecting citizen data

A justified recommendation on whether the Ethiopian government should proceed, and under what conditions

Key Terms
Copyright - A copyright is a type of intellectual property protection that
protects original works of authorship, which might include literary works,
music, art, and more
Digital citizenship- refers to the responsible use of technology by anyone
who uses computers, the Internet, and digital devices to engage with society
on any level.
Digital collaboration - means working together and accomplishing goals
while using digital tools and technology. It may consist of anything, such as
devices, programs, software, steps, or an entirely new technique.
Digital identity is an online or networked identity adopted or claimed in
cyberspace by an individual, organization, or electronic device.
Intellectual property right - refers to a range of intangible rights of ownership
as an asset such as a software program or designed information system
Information Systems – are interrelated components working together to
collect, process, store, and disseminate information to support decision-
making.
Information Technology (IT) - is the use of any computers, storage,
networking, and other physical devices, infrastructure, and processes to
create, process, store, secure, and exchange all forms of electronic data
Patent - a patent is a type of limited-duration protection that can be used
to protect inventions (or discoveries) that are new, non-obvious, and useful,
such as a new process, machine, article of manufacture, or composition of
matter.
Trade 	secret- 	refers to specific, private information that is important
to a business because it gives the business a competitive advantage in its
marketplace
Trademarks refer to phrases, words, or symbols that distinguish the source
of a product or service of one party from another.
Social media addiction is a behavioral addiction characterized by excessive
concern for social media, an insatiable want to access or use social media, and
a commitment of so much time and energy to social media that it interferes
with other crucial facets of life.
